i.The house

One room, two moods. Coffee while the town wakes up, then the lights go low and the glasses come out.

Say. V is an easy, unhurried place. Mornings are for Vietnam coffee, butter-kaya sourdough and the kind of breakfast your auntie would approve of.

Come evening, the bar takes over. Our signatures are built on familiar Asian flavours — sour plum, tea, lychee, star fruit — mixed with a light hand and served without fuss.

Before dusk

Black,white & kaya.

Cinnamon coffee, coconut coffee, a Sicilian with orange. Matcha by the slow pour. Focaccia, Ah Mo breakfast, nasi lemak for five ringgit.
